# Tobb Technologies - 30-Day Digital Marketing Campaign

## Overview
This individual project proposes a practical, low-cost social media campaign to improve Tobb Technologies visibility, explain its services clearly, and generate enquiries from potential clients. 

## Repository contents
- `proposal.md` - concise, referenced proposal explaining the opportunity, solution, implemetation, benefits and measurement.
- `marketing-assets/campaign-plan.md` - target audience, campaign messages, draft captions, calls to action and a four-week schedule. 
- `marketing-assets/tobb-technologies-social-promo.png` - sample promotional graphic created for this proposal. 
- `evidence/README.md` - evidence checklist and guidance for capturing proof of the completed work. 

## Intended audience
Small and growing businesses, entrepreneurs, and organizations that may need reliable technology or digital services. The exact audience should be refined after confirming Tobb Technologies' actual service offering and customer profile. 

## Busienss value 
Teh campaign gives the company reusable content, a consistent message, ande a simple way to track whether social media activity leads to genuine enquiries.
