# Marketing Asset: "Tech That Moves Your Business Forward"

## Campaign purpose
Introduce Tobb Technologies clearly, explain approved services, share useful information, and encourage prospective customers to make an enquiry. 

## Audience 
Provisional audience: small and growing bisnesses, entrepreneurs and organizations that may need technology or digital services. Confirm the audience witht the company before launch.

## Core message
Tobb Technologies can help busiensses explore practical technology solutions. Replace this general wording with a precise, approved value proposition once the company's services are confirmed.

## Suggested four-week schedule

| Week | Suggested content | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Post 1, Post 2 | Introduce the company and encourage discussion |
| 2 | Post 3 | Provide useful educational content |
| 3 | Post 4, Post 5 | Explain an approved service and answer questions |
| 4 | Post 6 plus a recap of the best-performing topic | Encourage enquiries and review results |

Suggested cadence: around three posts per week. Adapt it to the company's resources and the audience's response. Use the company's existing, approved social channels rather than creating new accoutns without permission.
