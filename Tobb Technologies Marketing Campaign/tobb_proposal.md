# Proposal: A 30-Day Social Media Campaign for Tobb Technologies

## 1. Opportunity and problem

Tobb Technologies can use a coordinated social media campaign to explain its services, improve brand visibility, and make it easier for potential clients to enquire. A social media presence alone does not guarantee new customers; the content needs to communicate a clear value proposition and direct interested people towards a next step. 

The opportunity is relevant in South Africa, where DataReportal estimated 29.1 million social media user identities in October 2025. This figure represents user identities rather than a conformed count of unique individuals, so it should be used as an indicator of potential reach, not as a promise of campaign performance (Kemp, 2026).

The initial observation behind this proposal is that a consistent set of service-focused posts may communicate the company's offering more clearly than occasional, unrelated posts. This is a proposal to test, not a claim that Tobb Technologies currently has a particular marketing problem. 

## 2. Target audience

The primary proposed audience is small and growing busiensses, entrepreneurs, or organizations seeking technology or digital support. The campaign should be adjusted once the company confirms its actual services, customer segments, location and preferred channels. 

## 3. Proposed solution

Create and test a 30-day social media campaign titled **“Tech That Moves Your Business Forward.”** It will use a small set of reusable promotional graphics, concise educational posts, service explainers, and clear calls to action. The sample poster is included in `marketing-assets/tobb-technologies-social-promo.png`; suggested copy and a four-week publishing plan are in `marketing-assets/campaign-plan.md`.

The campaign has three content themes:

1. **Service awareness:** explain approved services in plain language and show the kinds of problems they address. 
2. **Useful education:** share short tips or checklists relevant to the target audience. 
3. **Enquiry generation:** invite interested viewers to contact the comapny through an approved email address, website form or busienss messaging channel. 

The exact service labels shown in the sample graphic are placeholders. Tobb Technologies must verify and approve all servic edescriptions and contact details before publication. 

## 4. Justification and evidence

Social platforms offer a way to distribute content and engage audiences where they already spend time online. DataReportal's South Africa report estimated 29.1 million social media user identities in October 2025, equivalent to 44.9% of the population; its methodology warns that user identities may not represent unique people (Kemp, 2026). This supports testing social media as a potential awareness channel, but does not establish that a specific platfom or campaign will convert viewers into customers. 

The proposal therefore recommends a small, measurable pilot rather than assuming that more views will automatically lead to busienss growth. Performance should be assesed using reach, engagement, link clicks and qualified enquiries, alongside the time and cost required to produce the content. 

## 5. Implementation plan 

### Week 1 - Prepare
- Confirm the company's real services, audience, approved brand assets and contact route. 
- Review existing social channels and record baseline metrics. 
- Approve the sample poster and campaign copy. 

### Week 2 - Introduce 
- Publish an introductory post explaining what the company does. 
- Publish one serive explainer and one useful technology tip. 
- Respond professionally to relevant comments and enquiries. 

### Week 3 - Build trust
- Publish another educational post and a short frequently asked questions post. 
- Where available and approved, share a genuine project example or demonstration. Do not invent client results or testimonials.
- Invite questions from potential customers. 

### Week 4 - Convert and review
- Publish a clear enquiry-focused post.
- Review results against the baseline and campaign targets. 
- Identify the best-performing topics and recommend what to continue, change or stop. 

A suggested cadence is three posts per week. This is a starting point, not a claim that this frequency is optimal. It should be adjusted to available staff time and actual audience response. 

## 6. Expected benefits

- A more consistent and recognizable presentation of the company's services. 
- Reusable content that can be adapted across approved platforms. 
- More opportunities for prospective customers to discover the comapny and ask questions. 
- Better visibility into which topics and messages attract meaningful agreement. 
- A low-cost pilot that can inform a larger marketing plan. 

These are expected benefits, not guaranteed outcomes.

## 7. Success measures

Record a baseline before publishing, then review results weekly and at the end of 30 days. 

| Measure | How to assess it |
|---|---|
| Publishing consistency | Number of planned posts published compared with the schedule |
| Reach | Unique accounts reached, where the platform reports this metric |
| Engagement | Reactions, comments, shares and saves; compare rates consistently |
| Traffic | Clicks on the approved website or enquiry link |
| Enquiries | Number of relevant enquiries attributed to campaign content |
| Qualified leads | Enquiries that match the company's target customer and service offering |
| Cost and effort | Money spent and staff hours used to create and manage the campaign |

Set numeric targets after recording baseline performance and confirming available resources. Do not invent historical results. A useful initial outcome is to establish a baseline, publish consistently, and determine whether the campaign produces relevant enquries. 

## 8. Risks and controls

- **Incorrect service information:** obtain company approval before publication. 
- **Weak conversion path:** use one clear call to action and a working, monitored contact method.
- **Low engagement:** test different content themes and review results weekly. 
- **Privacy and reputation:** obtain permission before posting client information, photographs, logos or testimonials.
- **Misleading reporting:** distinguish reach and engagement from actual leads or sales, and report the measurement period clearly. 

## 9. Conclusion

A 30-day social media campaign is a practical, reviewable way for Tobb Technologies to test how clear service messaging and consistent content can support visibility and customer acquisition. The campaign combines a sample promotional graphic with draft posts, a schedule and measurable outcomes. Once the company confirms its services and contact details, the assets can be adapted for publication and the results used to guide future marketing decisions.
